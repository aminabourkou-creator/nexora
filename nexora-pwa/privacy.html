Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what personal data Nexora Studio collects, why, and how you can control it. Nexora Studio is a product of aminainfo, operated by Yamina Bourkou (Elche, Alicante, Spain), acting as the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. What we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Email address, nameAccount creation and sign-in (Firebase Authentication)
Username, bio, profile photoOptional — shown to other members of your rooms and friends
Chat messages, images, and files you sendCore function of the app: private room and direct-message chat
Room names/descriptions, friend listCore function: organizing your rooms and contacts
Basic device/browser infoStandard hosting logs (Vercel) for security and reliability

We do not collect payment information, government ID, or precise location.

2. How your data is used

  • To operate your account, rooms, friend connections, and chat history.
  • To power the optional @Nexora AI assistant: when you address a message to it, the recent conversation (and any image you ask it about) is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. This only happens when you explicitly use the AI feature.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Who processes your data

ServicePurpose
Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore)Account login and database storage
Google Gemini APIGenerates @Nexora AI assistant replies, only when you use it
VercelHosts the application

These providers may process data on servers outside the European Economic Area (e.g. the United States). Google and Vercel both rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and their own compliance frameworks to safeguard such transfers.

4. How long we keep it

Chat messages remain visible to members who are still in a room. If you leave a room and later rejoin, you will only see messages sent after you rejoined. If a room owner deletes a room, all of its messages and member records are permanently deleted. You can delete your account data at any time by contacting us (see Section 7).

5. Security

Access to your data is enforced through Firestore security rules: only members of a room can read its messages, and only the two participants of a direct conversation can read it. Passwords are never stored by us — Firebase Authentication handles them using industry-standard hashing.

6. Your rights under GDPR

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data (editable directly in your Profile).
  • Delete your account and associated data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.
